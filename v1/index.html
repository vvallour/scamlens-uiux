Anatomy of a second look

ILLUSTRATIVE URL
https://account-review.example/verify
DOMAIN
Who is this really?

Generic words like “account” or “review” are not proof of a connection to a familiar company.

PATH
What are you being asked to do?

A verification-style path is a reason to check the original message and confirm the sender.

CONTEXT
Does the request make sense?

Unexpected urgency or requests for personal details deserve an independent check.
